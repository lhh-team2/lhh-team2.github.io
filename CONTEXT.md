# Terminators Team Art

The visual identity of the Terminators FLL team, whose innovation project is eliminating cane toads as an invasive species.

## Language

### Artwork

**Badge**:
The team logo: a single Rider with a lasso plus the "TERMINATORS" wordmark, legible at sticker and favicon size.
_Avoid_: Logo (ambiguous with the Scene), icon

**Scene**:
The full illustration of all three Riders chasing Toad Bandits across the Australian outback, used for the pit banner and site header.
_Avoid_: Logo, poster

**Character Sheet**:
A plain-background reference image of one character from several angles, uploaded alongside prompts to keep that character consistent across images.
_Avoid_: Model sheet, turnaround

### Prompts

**Style Block**:
The shared art-style and hard-rules text applied to every generated image.

**Character Block**:
The canonical written description of one character, reused unchanged in every image that character appears in.

**Composition Block**:
The per-image text describing layout, pose, and framing; the only part written fresh for a new image.

### Characters

**Rider**:
One of three original, friendly robot cowboys, finished in black, grey, and silver metal tones, each with a distinct silhouette, its own accent color (bandana and eye glow), and the same brown cowboy hat. Named by finish: Silver Rider, Black Rider, Grey Rider. Never armed; carries only a lasso.
_Avoid_: Terminator (as a character look), cowboy, robot

**Robot Horse**:
The mechanical mount a Rider sits on; all three are identical brushed steel with copper joints.
_Avoid_: Horse, steed

**Toad Bandit**:
A recognizable cane toad (warty, large parotoid glands) dressed as a comically panicked outlaw with a bandit mask and a money bag.
_Avoid_: Frog, toad, villain
