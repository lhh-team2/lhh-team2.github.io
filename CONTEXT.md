# Terminators

The visual identity and browser game of the Terminators FLL team, whose innovation project is eliminating cane toads as an invasive species.

## Language

### Artwork

**Badge**:
The team logo: a single Rider with a lasso plus the "TERMINATORS" wordmark, legible at sticker and favicon size.
_Avoid_: Logo (ambiguous with the Scene), icon

**Scene**:
The full illustration of all three Riders chasing Toad Bandits across the Australian outback, used for the pit banner and site header.
_Avoid_: Logo, poster

**Character Sheet**:
A plain-background reference image of one character from several angles, uploaded alongside prompts to keep that character consistent across images.
_Avoid_: Model sheet, turnaround

### Prompts

**Style Block**:
The shared art-style and hard-rules text applied to every generated image.

**Character Block**:
The canonical written description of one character, reused unchanged in every image that character appears in.

**Composition Block**:
The per-image text describing layout, pose, and framing; the only part written fresh for a new image.

### Characters

**Rider**:
One of three original, friendly robot cowboys, finished in black, grey, and silver metal tones, each with a distinct silhouette, its own accent color (bandana and eye glow), and the same brown cowboy hat. Named by finish: Silver Rider, Black Rider, Grey Rider. Never armed; carries only a lasso.
_Avoid_: Terminator (as a character look), cowboy, robot

**Robot Horse**:
The mechanical mount a Rider sits on; all three are identical brushed steel with copper joints.
_Avoid_: Horse, steed

**Toad Bandit**:
A recognizable cane toad (warty, large parotoid glands) dressed as a comically panicked outlaw with a bandit mask and a money bag.
_Avoid_: Frog, toad, villain

### Game

**Run**:
One play-through of the game, ending when time runs out or every Toad Bandit in it has been caught. A Toad Bandit that escapes stays at large and can be chased down again.
_Avoid_: Game, round, level

**Lane**:
One of three horizontal tracks stacked on screen; the Rider moves between Lanes but never forward or back within one.
_Avoid_: Track, row

**Loot Bag**:
A bag of dropped money lying in a Lane, worth a hidden random amount, collected by riding over it.
_Avoid_: Money bag (that is the prop a Toad Bandit holds in the art), coin, pickup

**Hazard**:
An outback obstacle in a Lane (prickly pear, termite mound) that slows the Robot Horse when hit. Never costs Bounty.
_Avoid_: Obstacle, enemy

**Giddy-Up Meter**:
The timing bar below the Lanes whose swinging needle the player stops to speed up or slow down the Robot Horse.
_Avoid_: Speed bar, timing box

**Lasso Showdown**:
The zoomed-in button-mashing event that starts when the Rider closes on a Toad Bandit and decides whether it is caught or escapes.
_Avoid_: Minigame, boss fight, battle

**Bounty**:
The player's score in dollars, earned from Loot Bags and caught Toad Bandits.
_Avoid_: Score, points, money

**Codename**:
A randomly generated outback-themed name ("Dusty Wombat") that is a player's only identity on the leaderboard; players never type a name of their own. A returning player reclaims a Codename by typing it exactly.
_Avoid_: Random name, nickname, alias
