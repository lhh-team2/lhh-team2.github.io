## Why keep a blog?

Judges at competition ask what your team tried, what broke, and what you changed
because of it. A blog is the easiest way to have real answers ready instead of
trying to remember in November what you did back in August.

Short and frequent beats long and perfect. Three sentences after a meeting is a
great post.

## Good things to write about

- What you built or coded today, and whether it worked
- Something that broke and how you fixed it
- A design you tried and then threw away — judges love these
- Photos of your robot mid-build
- Outreach events and who you met

## Writing your own post

Open the `_posts` folder and copy `TEMPLATE-copy-this-file.md`. The instructions
are right at the top of it.

One rule matters more than the rest: **the file name has to start with the date**,
like `2026-08-15-we-built-the-arm.md`. Miss that and your post silently won't
appear.

Now go build something.
